# Unifinality: One-Page Briefing for Academic Peer Review

## Claim

**Unifinality** is a consciousness-first metaphysical proposal: one self-grounding awareness is the ultimate ground of reality, expressed through many real, irreducible, and bounded perspectives. The thesis does not deny physical mechanisms, neural dependence, individual agency, or scientific explanation. It denies that the plurality of perspectives establishes a plurality of independently self-grounding subjects, or that a functional description of brain processes has already explained why awareness exists.

## Problem addressed

The proposal addresses the Hard Problem of Consciousness: why physical or functional processes are accompanied by subjective experience—the fact that there is something it is like for a state to occur. Science can map neural correlates, causal mechanisms, access, report, attention, memory, and behavior. The unresolved ontological question is whether those descriptions, stated in structural-functional terms, explain the presence of awareness itself.

## Argument in four claims

| Claim | Brief rationale |
|---|---|
| **1. Awareness is irreducible in inquiry.** | Every measurement, theory, denial, and observation appears within awareness; inquiry cannot stand outside it to explain it away. This establishes epistemic, not yet complete ontological, priority. |
| **2. An ultimate ground should be self-grounding.** | Infinite regress, brute contingency, and unexplained ultimate plurality leave the demand for grounding unresolved. Awareness is proposed as self-present rather than inferred from an external witness. |
| **3. The ultimate ground is one.** | If two ultimate subjects differ, their difference appears to require a prior relation, principle, or field in which it holds. Unifinality argues that such a prior condition points to a more fundamental unity. |
| **4. Plurality is real differentiation.** | Embodiment, memory, informational boundaries, and causal history yield real perspectives with privacy and autonomy; they do not entail final metaphysical separation. |

## Position relative to alternatives

Unifinality shares panpsychism’s resistance to the radical emergence of experience from a wholly non-experiential base. It differs from standard micropsychism by beginning with one ground rather than many fundamental micro-subjects, aiming to avoid the combination and subject-summing problems.[1] It differs from illusionism by treating phenomenal appearing as real; an illusion is still a mode of appearing and cannot eliminate the fact that anything appears.[2] It differs from reductive physicalism by accepting physical explanation while disputing that causal-functional success entails ontological exhaustiveness.

## Scientific boundary

The thesis is **not** presented as a scientific result already established by quantum mechanics or neuroscience. Bell-test and entanglement research challenges simple local hidden-variable models, but it does not prove one consciousness or a consciousness-caused observer effect.[3] Neuroscience demonstrates that brain organization conditions the contents and capacities of experience; it does not, without additional metaphysical argument, demonstrate that the brain produces awareness from non-awareness.

## Scholarly contribution and questions for review

Unifinality offers a non-aggregative account of consciousness: one awareness differentiated into many perspectives. Its potential contribution is to reframe the Hard Problem from “How does non-awareness generate awareness?” to “How does one self-grounding awareness become locally structured as bounded perspectives?”

Review is sought on four questions: **(1)** Does the grounding argument validly move from awareness’s epistemic irreducibility to ontological priority? **(2)** Does the one-ground argument avoid merely stipulating unity? **(3)** Can differentiation explain privacy, agency, memory boundaries, and intersubjective disagreement without covertly reintroducing many ultimate subjects? **(4)** Does the thesis yield explanatory advantages over cosmopsychism, panpsychism, illusionism, and physicalism sufficient to justify its added commitments?

## Ethical guardrail

No unity claim licenses the erasure of individuals. In Unifinality, consent, privacy, autonomy, accountability, and the reality of suffering remain non-negotiable. The thesis’s ethical implication is not that differences disappear, but that difference cannot serve as an alibi for indifference.

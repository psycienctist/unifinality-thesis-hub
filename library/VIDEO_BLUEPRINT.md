# Unifinality Narrated Slide Video — Production Blueprint

## Delivery premise

The production is a **linear 16:9 narrated presentation video**, not a photorealistic film. It preserves the existing Unifinality slide deck as the central visual record and adds only restrained, abstract motion between selected conceptual turns. The visual language remains deep indigo, electric blue, warm gold, and monospaced philosophical-editorial typography. There is **no music** and no sound effects. The voice is the sole audio element.

## Narration identity

| Attribute | Locked direction |

| **Voice selection** | Charon — informative male voice, directed toward seriousness rather than announcer energy |
| **Language** | English (United States) |
| **Delivery pace** | Measured documentary cadence, approximately 2.1 words per second, with intentional pauses on the central propositions |
| **Mix** | Narration only, full foreground volume; no BGM; no SFX |

## Visual grammar

| Layer | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Slides** | Existing 16:9 Unifinality deck rendered in full frame. Subtle 1–2% slow push-in or lateral drift only; no content changes. |
| **Transitions** | 450–700 ms dissolved transitions with a slight blue-gold luminance wash. Never use flashy wipes or motion that competes with the argument. |
| **Atmospheric interludes** | Abstract, generated only with deterministic HTML/CSS: slowly differentiating pinpoints, a single luminous field becoming many points, and several points resolving into one coherent field. These are illustrative, never described as empirical evidence. |
| **Evidence discipline** | The quantum passage names entanglement and Bell-test findings as a challenge to naïve local separability; neither it nor the interludes may visually imply that physics proves one consciousness. |
| **Text treatment** | The deck remains the source of all visible substantive text. Interludes contain no text, labels, graphs, quantum formulas, people, or logos. |

## Transition rationale

The three interludes create breathing room after the materialist/quantum boundary, after the argument for one ground, and before the ethical consequence. Each has a specific narrative role: from physical non-separability to grounding; from unity to differentiated perspective; and from argument to ethical demand. They never purport to visualize particles, consciousness, or proof.

## Quality gates

1. Generate all narration WAV assets from the locked script before they are placed in the composition.
2. Derive exact scene duration from generated audio files rather than the script estimates.
3. Render a contact sheet or midpoint frames of every slide scene and manually inspect for legibility, crop safety, and transition consistency.
4. Run structural and runtime checks on the composition.
5. Open a final preview for review before rendering the final MP4.
6. Verify the final MP4 contains all 12 slides, all 3 interludes, and audible narration with no music.

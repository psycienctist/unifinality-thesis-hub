# Enhancement Research Sources

## Quantum information science
NIST states that quantum effects set fundamental limits on the precision of physical measurements and describes quantum information science as a field joining quantum physics and information theory. This supports cautious claims about the scientific importance of quantum information and measurement; it does not support claims that entanglement establishes consciousness, cosmic mind, human interconnectedness, or Unifinality.

Source: [NIST, Quantum information science](https://www.nist.gov/quantum-information-science)

## Neuroscience of consciousness
The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on the neuroscience of consciousness distinguishes neural correlates from stronger causal or explanatory sufficiency claims, discusses report and no-report methods, and notes that neuroscience faces limits in explaining subjective experience because of the explanatory gap. This supports the requested language about rich brain/body dependencies, empirical constraints, and the absence of a consensus answer to why physical processes are accompanied by subjective presence.

Source: [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, The Neuroscience of Consciousness](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness-neuroscience/)

## Planned citation policy
The site will distinguish established empirical claims, interpretive claims, and Unifinality’s own metaphysical proposal. Citations will be attached to the specific claim they support rather than used as authority for broader conclusions.
